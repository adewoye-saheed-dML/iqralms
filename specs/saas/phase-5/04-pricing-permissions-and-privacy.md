# Task 5.4 — Pricing Permissions and Privacy

Permissions must be evaluated in academy context. A lead in Academy A must not gain pricing authority in Academy B merely because the global user role is `lead`.

Students may see only their own agreements within an academy where they are active members.

Leads may manage pricing only for academies where they have the required active membership and authorization.

Private pricing notes and internal approval details must not be exposed to unauthorized users.

Do not expand parent pricing visibility in this phase; defer it to an explicit future requirement.
